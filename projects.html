Swingfidi

swingfidi.com

My co-worker challenged me that Spider-Man wouldn't be able to swing around SF. So I built this to prove him wrong.

Swingfidi is fully live. The Financial District and more of the city's major landmarks are all mapped out, so you can actually swing, glide and zip your way through it.

Wild Quest

wildquest.site

A Wild West–themed productivity site. You hop on quests to gamify your time and hit peak productivity, and the trail keeps you honest:

  • random encounters
  • a hunger bar — feed her or she slows
  • a prairie dog that checks in on your engagement
  • a shop for equipment, companions, and even a six-shooter

the goal: finish the trail, fight the Wendigo, and make it to the settlement.

the trail runs 18,000 miles

Onvoice

still building

Onvoice was my first passion project and mini-startup: ambient recording. I went through Stanford Ventures with it, connecting with people and working on it consistently, and I'm still actively building.

We worked on the UI/UX, and Joshua (co-founder and friend!) built most of the infrastructure — efficient RAG retrieval, our dual-ASR system, and the rest of the technical work. He's extremely smart and full of character, and I'm grateful we get to build Onvoice together.
